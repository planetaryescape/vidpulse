# VidPulse

Worth It? Maybe? Skip?
Know before you watch.

AI watches YouTube videos so you don't have to. Get a verdict before you commit.

## How it works
1. Open any YouTube video
2. Sidebar shows summary, scores, verdict
3. Like/dislike teaches it your taste

## What you get
- 5 scores: productivity, educational, entertainment, inspiring, creative
- Chat with video — ask questions without watching
- Timestamps in answers — click to jump
- Notes sync to timeline
- Block junk during focus hours

## Privacy trade-off
BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). Your OpenRouter API key stays on your device. We never see your data.

## Get started
1. Get API key → openrouter.ai/keys
2. Install extension
3. Open a YouTube video
